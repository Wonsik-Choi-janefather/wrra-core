## Version rule

Domain Profile revisions do not automatically revise WRRA Core 1.0. Physics reinforcement with string-theoretic microphysics, new biological data, new AI agents, improved residue measurements, or a new domain mapping remains a Domain Profile change unless it modifies a constitutional invariant or required execution element of the Core.
